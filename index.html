Data preparation

Standardization

Converted the Income field from text (e.g. "$58.138,00") to numbers using Find & Replace and Text to Columns, and standardized the registration dates.

Missing values

24 records (1.1%) had no income. Filled with the median income (about $51.4K) using the MEDIAN function to avoid skewing the distribution.

Invalid entries

Filtered out the marital status values "Absurd" and "YOLO", merged "Alone" into "Single", and flagged extreme birth years and income values as outliers.

Feature engineering

Removed two constant columns (Z_CostContact, Z_Revenue). Added total spend and household children columns with formulas for segmentation.

Key findings and recommendations

Prioritize high-income customers

The top income quartile responds 2.5x more often and spends nearly 17x more than the bottom quartile. Allocate premium campaign budget to this segment.

Time offers right after a purchase

Customers who bought within the last 25 days respond 3.6x more often than those inactive for over 75 days. Trigger campaigns shortly after each transaction.

Build offers around wine and meat

These two categories account for 78% of spend. Wine and meat bundles are well suited to customers without children, the highest-spending segment.

Review Campaign 2 before repeating it

Only 1.3% accepted, compared with roughly 7% for comparable campaigns. The offer and audience targeting should be reassessed.
